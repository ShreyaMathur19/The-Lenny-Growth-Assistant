# Ship 30 for 30 Long-Form Skill

Purpose: turn transcript-grounded research into an approximately 1,250-word, skimmable digital essay.

Writing principles encoded from Ship 30 for 30 guidance:
- Define a specific reader, problem, promise, and useful outcome before writing.
- Use a headline that makes the subject and benefit clear.
- Open the essay and major sections with a strong single-sentence opener.
- Prefer skimmability: descriptive subheads, short paragraphs, lists where compression helps, and selective bold emphasis.
- Vary writing rhythm rather than making every paragraph the same length.
- Maintain a high rate of revelation: each section should introduce a useful idea, example, distinction, or action.
- Use a clear progression: Hook -> problem/tension -> grounded insights -> practical framework -> specific takeaway.
- Avoid empty motivational filler and generic business advice.

Grounding contract:
- Every factual/product claim must be supported by the supplied transcript context and cited [S#].
- Do not invent stories or quotes.
- If the transcript evidence is too thin for a ~1,250 word essay, say so rather than padding with unsupported claims.
